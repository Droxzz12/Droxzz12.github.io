My Future Direction

I want to combine my background in economics with technology. Knowing how to build software is great, but knowing how to turn it into a business is my goal.

Having specialized in entrepreneurship during my high school studies, I've always viewed technology through a business lens. Whether it is structuring a business plan for a startup or designing digital marketing materials, I realize that clean, effective front-end development is what ultimately connects a great product to its customers.

During my time in the HBO-ICT program, I plan to bridge this gap between code and commerce. I am not just learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to build static pages; I am learning them to create scalable, user-friendly web applications that solve real-world market problems. My ultimate direction is to become a front-end developer who doesn't just write code, but builds thriving digital experiences that make business sense.

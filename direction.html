My Future Direction

Bridging Economics and Technology
Having specialized in Entrepreneurship during my secondary education, I've always viewed technology through a business lens. Whether it is structuring a business plan or designing digital marketing materials, I realize that clean, effective front-end development is what ultimately connects a great product to its customers. My goal is to turn software into a business asset.

Data Science vs. Software Engineering
Looking ahead in the HBO-ICT program, I must eventually choose a specialization. Data Science is incredibly valuable for analyzing market trends, predicting user behavior, and driving business decisions with raw metrics. However, analyzing data is only half the battle. My true passion lies in building the actual tools that users interact with.

My Chosen Path: Software Engineering
Because I want to actively construct digital experiences, my future direction is firmly set on the Software Engineering track. I am not just learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to build static pages; my objective is to master modern web architecture to create scalable, user-friendly applications that solve real-world market problems. I aim to become a software engineer who doesn't just write code, but builds thriving platforms that make business sense.

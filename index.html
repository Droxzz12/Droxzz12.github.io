From Economics to IT

Why HBO-ICT at HZ is My Next Step

I have always been driven by how businesses operate and grow, but I realized the real power lies in building the systems behind them. I chose the IT field because I want to transition from just using digital tools to actively developing them.

Whether it is solving logical puzzles, writing clean code, or structuring a complex project, I thrive on hands-on problem-solving. I chose HZ University because its practical, real-world approach perfectly matches my trial-and-error learning style.

My SMART Goals for Year 1:

  • Academic: Secure the 45 EC Study Advice boundary by passing all core programming exams in every Quarter.
  • Technical: Build and deploy a fully responsive, multi-page web application using HTML, CSS, and Flexbox by the end of Semester 1.
  • Career: Actively explore both Software Engineering and Data Science modules to finalize my specialization choice by Quarter 4.

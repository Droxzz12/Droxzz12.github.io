My Programming Experience

To be honest, I am starting fresh with programming. I have been playing around with some basic codes, trying to understand how the logic around it works.

Right now, my primary focus is getting completely comfortable navigating Visual Studio Code and understanding the foundational rules of HTML and CSS. I spend a lot of time seeing what breaks when I change a line of code, which has been the best way for me to actually grasp the mechanics of web structure and styling.

Moving forward in the HBO-ICT program, I am excited to transition from these basic static codes to writing dynamic JavaScript and properly pushing all my functioning projects up to GitHub. I know I am at the very beginning of this journey, but I am determined to build my technical logic step by step.

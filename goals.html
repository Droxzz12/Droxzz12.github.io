Goals & SWOT Analysis

To ensure my study success in the HBO-ICT program, I have outlined two primary SMART goals for this year. To achieve them, I conducted a SWOT analysis to understand what internal and external factors will impact my journey.

My SMART Goals

  • Goal 1: Pass the 45 EC Study Advice boundary by completing all core programming and project assignments with a grade of 7.0 or higher by the end of Quarter 4.
  • Goal 2: Transition from trial-and-error HTML/CSS coding to writing functional, dynamic JavaScript by building a fully responsive web application by the end of Semester 1.

SWOT Analysis

Strengths (Internal) Weaknesses (Internal)
  • Strong entrepreneurial background; I understand the business logic behind the software.
  • High discipline and consistency, built through years of dedicated gym training.
  • Hands-on problem solver who learns by doing.
  • Starting fresh with programming, little coding experience.
  • My trial-and-error learning style can sometimes be time-consuming when debugging complex issues.
Opportunities (External) Threats (External)
  • HZ's project-based curriculum fits my practical learning style perfectly.
  • Collaborating with more experienced classmates during group projects.
  • Massive industry demand for developers who understand business needs.
  • The steep learning curve of moving from HTML to complex back-end logic.
  • Balancing the heavy study workload (at least 40 hours/week) with my personal gym routine and downtime.

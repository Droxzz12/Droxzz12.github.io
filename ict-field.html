The ICT Field

As a first-year ICT student, my primary mission this year is to connect my passion for clean front-end design with effective, real-world problem solving.

To achieve this, I am continuously developing this responsive portfolio to showcase my technical growth and document my upcoming project case studies.

Because I know the best development happens as a team, I am also pushing myself to break out of solo troubleshooting by actively engaging with my classmates and sharing knowledge when I face technical problems.

Ultimately, this platform will serve as a transparent record of my journey, reflecting both the digital experiences I build and the teamwork skills I develop along the way.

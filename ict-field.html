The ICT Field of Work

The Evolving Tech Landscape
The ICT field of work is incredibly vast, ranging from massive corporate tech giants to agile, innovative startups. As someone with an entrepreneurial mindset, I am particularly interested in how software engineering roles fit into business growth. The industry is currently experiencing massive demand for developers who don't just write code, but understand how to build digital products that solve real-world problems.

Roles and Opportunities
During our workshops, we are given the opportunity to research different ICT companies and the roles within them. Whether functioning as a Front-End Developer crafting user experiences, or a Data Scientist analyzing business metrics, the opportunities are endless. My goal is to use this year to research which specific sector of the tech industry aligns best with my desire to build scalable web applications.

Why Teamwork Matters
One major takeaway from studying the ICT field is that modern software development is rarely a solo job. The best development happens in cross-functional teams using Agile methodologies. I am pushing myself to break out of solo troubleshooting by actively engaging with my classmates, preparing myself for the collaborative environment of professional IT companies.

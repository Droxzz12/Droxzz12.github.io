Why IT?

My Motivation & Expectations
Coming from a background in entrepreneurship, I have always looked at how systems operate and generate value. I quickly realized that IT is the backbone of the modern economy. My motivation for entering the IT field is to shift from being a consumer of digital tools to a creator. While the career stability and financial growth in the tech industry are excellent, my true drive is to build scalable web applications that solve real-world business problems. I expect the field to be challenging and constantly evolving, which is exactly why I chose it—I want a career that forces me to continuously adapt and learn.

Why HZ University & The HBO-ICT Programme
I specifically chose the HBO-ICT programme at HZ University of Applied Sciences because of its hands-on, practical approach. I am a trial-and-error learner who prefers building and executing code over just reading theoretical concepts. The project-based environment at HZ aligns perfectly with how I learn best.

What I Want to Learn & Achieve
During this programme, I want to master front-end and back-end web development, specifically focusing on how to structure clean, maintainable software. Ultimately, my goal is to become a highly skilled IT specialist capable of engineering robust digital platforms and potentially leading my own tech ventures in the future.
